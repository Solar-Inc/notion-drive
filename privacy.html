Notion Drive Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Notion Drive is a Chrome extension that lets users use their own Notion workspace as an encrypted file drive. This Privacy Policy explains what data the extension handles, why it is needed, where it is stored, and how it is used.

Summary: Notion Drive does not sell user data, does not use user data for advertising, and does not operate a separate analytics or tracking service. Data handled by the extension is used only to provide the extension's file-drive, encryption, profile, synchronization, upload, and download features.

1. Data handled by Notion Drive

Authentication information

The extension may handle information required to connect to and unlock a user's Notion Drive, including:

  • Notion integration tokens provided by the user.
  • Encryption passwords entered by the user.
  • Encrypted credentials and encrypted profile configuration.
  • Encryption-related key material required to unlock the user's encrypted drive.

Encryption passwords are used locally to derive or unlock encryption keys. Notion Drive does not transmit the user's encryption password to a developer-operated server.

Website and file content

To provide drive functionality, the extension may access content stored in the user's selected Notion workspace, including Notion pages, blocks, file metadata, folder metadata, and files that the user chooses to upload, download, preview, organize, or delete.

2. How data is used

User data is used only to provide Notion Drive's core features, including:

  • Connecting to the user's selected Notion workspace.
  • Reading and managing drive-related Notion pages and blocks.
  • Uploading, downloading, moving, renaming, previewing, searching, favoriting, and deleting files.
  • Encrypting files before storage and decrypting files locally when accessed.
  • Maintaining local drive metadata, index state, cache, and profile settings.
  • Synchronizing encrypted profile information through Chrome Sync when the user uses that feature.
  • Remembering this computer when the user enables that option.

3. Where data is stored

Depending on the feature being used, Notion Drive may store data in:

  • The user's own Notion workspace.
  • Chrome extension storage, including chrome.storage.local, chrome.storage.session, and chrome.storage.sync.
  • Browser-managed local storage used for temporary large-file processing, such as Origin Private File System storage.

Sensitive profile information stored through Chrome Sync is encrypted by Notion Drive before synchronization when applicable to the feature.

4. Network access

Notion Drive communicates only with services required for its core functionality, including:

  • api.notion.com for the Notion API.
  • *.notion-static.com for Notion-hosted file content.
  • *.amazonaws.com when Notion provides temporary file storage URLs backed by Amazon S3.

The extension does not request access to arbitrary websites and does not collect the user's general browsing history.

5. Remote code

Notion Drive does not execute remotely hosted JavaScript or WebAssembly. Executable code used by the extension is included in the extension package. Data retrieved from Notion or file-storage endpoints is treated as data, not executable code.

6. Data sharing and sale

Notion Drive does not:

  • Sell user data.
  • Use user data for advertising or behavioral profiling.
  • Use user data for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose.
  • Use user data for credit scoring, lending, or financial eligibility decisions.

Data may be transmitted to Notion and related file-storage infrastructure only as necessary to perform the actions requested by the user.

7. Analytics and tracking

Notion Drive does not include a developer-operated analytics, advertising, or cross-site tracking service.

8. Data retention and deletion

Users can remove local extension data by deleting profiles, clearing extension storage, or uninstalling the extension. Data stored in the user's Notion workspace remains under the user's control and can be removed from Notion by the user.

Chrome Sync data is managed through the user's Chrome account and Chrome synchronization settings. Uninstalling the extension may not by itself immediately remove every value synchronized by Chrome.

9. Security

Notion Drive is designed to encrypt drive files before storage and to decrypt them locally when accessed. Users are responsible for protecting their Notion integration token, encryption password, Chrome account, and devices.

If required encryption credentials are lost, encrypted files may become unrecoverable.

10. Third-party services

Notion Drive depends on third-party services including Notion and Chrome. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

11. Children's privacy

Notion Drive is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Notion Drive's functionality or data practices change. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.

13. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use the support contact or support page associated with the Notion Drive listing on the Chrome Web Store.

Notion Drive is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Notion Labs, Inc.

Notion Drive 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 9 月 13 日

Notion Drive 是一款 Chrome 擴充功能，讓使用者可以將自己的 Notion 工作區作為加密檔案空間。 本政策說明擴充功能會處理哪些資料、處理原因、儲存位置與用途。

摘要：Notion Drive 不販售使用者資料、不將使用者資料用於廣告， 也沒有使用開發者自建的分析或追蹤服務。擴充功能處理的資料只用於提供檔案管理、 加密、Profile、同步、上傳及下載功能。

1. Notion Drive 會處理的資料

驗證資訊

為了連接與解鎖使用者的 Notion Drive，擴充功能可能處理：

  • 使用者自行提供的 Notion Integration Token。
  • 使用者輸入的加密密碼。
  • 加密後的憑證與 Profile 設定。
  • 解鎖加密 Drive 所需的相關金鑰資料。

加密密碼只用於本機衍生或解鎖加密金鑰。Notion Drive 不會將使用者的加密密碼 傳送至開發者自行營運的伺服器。

網站與檔案內容

為了提供 Drive 功能，擴充功能可能存取使用者指定 Notion 工作區內的內容， 包括 Notion 頁面、Block、檔案 Metadata、資料夾 Metadata，以及使用者選擇 上傳、下載、預覽、整理或刪除的檔案。

2. 資料用途

使用者資料只會用於提供 Notion Drive 的核心功能，包括：

  • 連接使用者指定的 Notion 工作區。
  • 讀取與管理 Drive 相關的 Notion Page 與 Block。
  • 上傳、下載、移動、重新命名、預覽、搜尋、加入最愛及刪除檔案。
  • 在儲存前加密檔案，並在使用時於本機解密。
  • 維護本機 Drive Metadata、索引、快取與 Profile 設定。
  • 使用相關功能時，透過 Chrome Sync 同步加密後的 Profile 資訊。
  • 使用者啟用「記住這台電腦」時保存必要的本機解鎖資訊。

3. 資料儲存位置

依照使用功能不同，Notion Drive 可能將資料儲存在：

  • 使用者自己的 Notion 工作區。
  • Chrome 擴充功能儲存空間，包括 chrome.storage.local、chrome.storage.session 與 chrome.storage.sync。
  • 瀏覽器管理的本機儲存空間，例如大檔案暫存所使用的 Origin Private File System。

透過 Chrome Sync 儲存的敏感 Profile 資訊，在相關功能適用時會先由 Notion Drive 加密後再同步。

4. 網路存取

Notion Drive 只會連線至核心功能所需的服務，包括：

  • api.notion.com：存取 Notion API。
  • *.notion-static.com：取得由 Notion 提供的檔案內容。
  • *.amazonaws.com：Notion 提供以 Amazon S3 為基礎的暫時檔案網址時，用於檔案上傳或下載。

擴充功能不要求任意網站存取權限，也不收集使用者的一般瀏覽紀錄。

5. 遠端程式碼

Notion Drive 不執行遠端託管的 JavaScript 或 WebAssembly。擴充功能所需的可執行程式碼 均包含在擴充功能套件內。從 Notion 或檔案儲存端點取得的內容只會作為資料處理，不會作為程式碼執行。

6. 資料分享與販售

Notion Drive 不會：

  • 販售使用者資料。
  • 將使用者資料用於廣告或行為分析。
  • 將使用者資料用於與擴充功能單一用途無關的目的。
  • 將使用者資料用於信用評分、貸款或金融資格判定。

僅在執行使用者要求的操作時，必要資料才可能傳送至 Notion 與相關檔案儲存基礎設施。

7. Analytics 與追蹤

Notion Drive 不包含由開發者營運的 Analytics、廣告或跨網站追蹤服務。

8. 資料保留與刪除

使用者可以透過刪除 Profile、清除擴充功能儲存資料或解除安裝擴充功能來移除本機資料。 儲存在使用者 Notion 工作區中的資料仍由使用者控制，並可由使用者自行在 Notion 中刪除。

Chrome Sync 資料由使用者的 Chrome 帳號與同步設定管理。解除安裝擴充功能本身， 不一定會立即刪除所有曾透過 Chrome 同步的資料。

9. 安全性

Notion Drive 的設計是在檔案儲存前完成加密，並於使用時在本機解密。 使用者應自行妥善保護 Notion Integration Token、加密密碼、Chrome 帳號與裝置。

如果遺失必要的加密憑證，加密檔案可能無法復原。

10. 第三方服務

Notion Drive 依賴 Notion 與 Chrome 等第三方服務。這些服務如何處理資料， 由其各自的隱私權政策與服務條款規範。

11. 兒童隱私

Notion Drive 並非以刻意蒐集兒童個人資訊為目的而設計。

12. 政策更新

當 Notion Drive 的功能或資料處理方式改變時，本政策可能更新。 頁面頂端的日期代表目前最新版本。

13. 聯絡方式

如有隱私權相關問題，請透過 Chrome Web Store 上 Notion Drive 商品頁所提供的支援聯絡方式或支援頁面與開發者聯絡。

Notion Drive 為獨立開發專案，與 Notion Labs, Inc. 無官方隸屬或背書關係。
